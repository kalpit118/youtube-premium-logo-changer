Get started

Install in 3 steps

No Chrome Web Store required. Works on any Chromium browser.

  1. 1

    Download the Extension

    Head to the GitHub Releases page and download the latest .zip file. Extract it anywhere on your computer.

    Download .zip
  2. 2

    Load Unpacked in Your Browser

    Open your browser extensions page, enable Developer Mode, click Load unpacked, and select the extracted folder.

    chrome://extensions/ brave://extensions/ edge://extensions/
  3. 3

    Pin & Enjoy

    Click the puzzle icon in your browser toolbar, find the extension, and pin it. Open YouTube — your logo is now Premium!

    Click the extension icon anytime to open the popup and tweak settings live.
